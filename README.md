🌊 URF – AI-Based Water Pollution Monitoring System using NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano

Project Overview

Water pollution is one of the major environmental challenges affecting human health, aquatic ecosystems, and biodiversity. Traditional water quality monitoring methods often require manual sample collection and laboratory testing, which can be time-consuming and may not provide continuous monitoring.

The URF (Undergraduate Research Fellowship) Project – Water Pollution Monitoring System aims to develop a smart, technology-driven solution for monitoring water quality using the NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano.

The system focuses on collecting and analysing water quality parameters to identify possible pollution levels and provide useful information for environmental monitoring.

🎯 Objectives

- To develop a smart water pollution monitoring system.
- To use NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano for edge computing.
- To monitor important water quality parameters.
- To analyse collected sensor data.
- To identify abnormal water quality conditions.
- To provide timely information about potential water pollution.
- To support environmental protection and water resource management.

⚙️ Working Methodology

1. Data Collection: Water samples are monitored using suitable water quality sensors.
2. Sensor Interfacing: Sensors collect water quality readings and transmit them to the processing unit.
3. Data Processing: NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano processes the collected sensor information.
4. Data Analysis: The readings are analysed to identify unusual water quality conditions.
5. Pollution Identification: The system identifies potential pollution based on selected water quality parameters and defined thresholds.
6. Result Display: The analysed information is displayed to help users understand the water quality status.

🧠 Role of NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano

The NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano acts as the main edge-computing platform.

Its potential roles include:

- Processing sensor readings.
- Running Python-based data analysis.
- Supporting AI/ML-based water quality classification.
- Enabling local processing without depending entirely on cloud computing.
- Supporting future real-time monitoring and alert systems.
